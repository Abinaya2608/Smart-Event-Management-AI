1. Privacy Policy

How information is handled

1.1 Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the platform may process information such as:

  • Basic account details, such as your name and email address.
  • Event information, including event names, dates, venues, schedules, and planning details.
  • Messages or feedback submitted through contact forms.
  • Technical information required to operate and troubleshoot the application.

The information actually collected depends on the features implemented and enabled in the current version of the platform.

1.2 How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain event management features.
  • Display event information and planning summaries.
  • Respond to user enquiries and feedback.
  • Improve application functionality and usability.
  • Identify technical issues and help protect the platform.

1.3 Data Storage and Security

Information is handled through the application's configured storage and processing mechanisms. Reasonable precautions should be used to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no web application or method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid submitting passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information through general-purpose forms.

1.4 Sharing of Information

Personal information should not be sold or disclosed to unrelated third parties for their own purposes. Information may be processed by hosting providers or other services configured to operate the platform, where applicable, or disclosed when required by law.

1.5 Cookies and Technical Data

The application may use session cookies or similar technical mechanisms to support essential functionality, such as maintaining a user's session. Additional analytics or tracking technologies should only be described here if they are actually implemented.

1.6 Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the relevant functionality, maintain records, or meet applicable legal requirements. Requests to access, correct, or delete information can be directed to the project team, subject to applicable requirements and the capabilities of the application.

1.7 Third-Party Services

Some features may depend on third-party services, such as hosting platforms, external APIs, or data-processing tools. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms. Their handling of information is governed by their applicable policies.

1.8 Children's Privacy

The platform is not specifically designed to collect children's personal information. Users should not submit another person's personal information without appropriate permission or a lawful basis.

1.9 Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the platform's features or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
